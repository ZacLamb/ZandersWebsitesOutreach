When to Call an Emergency Plumber

  • No heat or no hot water
  • A burst or leaking pipe
  • Water in the basement
  • A boiler or furnace that won't start
  • A backed-up main drain
  • The smell of oil or exhaust near the burner

Emergency Plumber FAQs

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Emergency plumbing and no-heat service is available around the clock.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Shut off the main water valve, turn off power to anything wet, and call us.

My boiler stopped working. What should I check?

Check the thermostat, the emergency switch and, for oil heat, the tank level. If it still won't run, call us.
