Overview

Tile Installation in [[CITY]] & the [[CITY]] Area

A tile job is only as good as what’s behind it. We waterproof showers properly, get the floor flat and solid, then set the tile straight so it doesn’t crack or leak down the road.

We prep and waterproof first, lay it out so the cuts make sense, then set and grout it.
