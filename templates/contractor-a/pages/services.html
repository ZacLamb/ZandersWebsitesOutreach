Our Services

From backyard ADUs to a rotted step — inside, outside, big or small.

01

ADUs & In-Law Apartments

An ADU (accessory dwelling unit) is a second, smaller home on your property — a backyard cottage, an apartment over the garage, or an in-law suite in the basement or an addition. It’s a place for Mom or Dad, a grown kid who needs a start, or rental income that helps pay the mortgage. We build them from the ground up: foundation, framing, utilities, finishes and the permits in between.

  • Detached backyard cottages
  • Garage conversions and apartments over the garage
  • In-law suites in additions and basements
  • Foundations, framing, roofing and siding

General Contracting

Big jobs go sideways when nobody’s in charge. We run the whole thing — permits, subs, materials, schedule — and the owner is the one you call. No runaround, no finger-pointing between trades.

  • Whole-home and multi-room renovations
  • Additions, bump-outs and dormers
  • Permit coordination with your town building department
  • Trade scheduling — electrical, plumbing, HVAC, drywall
More on General Contracting

Home Remodeling

Old bathroom, unfinished basement, a wall that needs to come down — we do the demo, fix what we find behind it and build it back solid. Real work, done right, at a fair price.

  • Bathroom remodels
  • Basement finishing
  • Open-concept conversions and wall removal
  • Flooring, trim and door packages

Kitchens & Cabinetry

Good cabinets hung crooked are bad cabinets. We tear out the old kitchen, fix the walls and floor, and install new cabinets that are level, tight and built to take a beating from a busy family.

  • Full kitchen remodels
  • Cabinet installation and replacement
  • Islands and pantries
  • Cabinet trim and crown
More on Kitchens & Cabinetry

Carpentry & Trim

Carpentry is what we do every day. Framing walls, hanging doors, running trim, fixing rotted stairs and building shelves — the everyday wood work that keeps a house solid and looking right.

  • Framing and structural repairs
  • Baseboard, crown and casing
  • Interior and exterior doors
  • Wainscoting and board-and-batten
More on Carpentry & Trim

Custom Closets

Not enough room for your stuff? We build closets, mudroom cubbies and pantry shelving that actually hold up — solid shelves and rods, built to fit the space you have.

  • Walk-in closet build-outs
  • Reach-in closet systems
  • Drawers and shelving
  • Mudroom lockers and storage walls

Tile Installation

A tile job is only as good as what’s behind it. We waterproof showers properly, get the floor flat and solid, then set the tile straight so it doesn’t crack or leak down the road.

  • Walk-in and curbless showers
  • Bathroom and kitchen floors
  • Backsplashes and feature walls
  • Porcelain, ceramic and stone
More on Tile Installation

Decks & Outdoor Living

Decks are a big part of what we do. Footings dug and poured right, framing that’s built to code and decking that holds up to snow, rain and cookouts. New builds, rebuilds and repairs.

  • Composite and PVC decking
  • Pressure-treated and cedar decks
  • Aluminum, cable and composite railings
  • Multi-level decks and stairs

Siding

Siding keeps the weather out. We strip the old stuff, fix any rot underneath, wrap and flash it properly and put up new siding and trim that keeps water out for years.

  • Fiber cement siding
  • Vinyl siding
  • Cedar shingle and clapboard
  • PVC and composite trim

Window Replacement

  • Exterior doors and patio doors
  • Interior casing and stool trim
