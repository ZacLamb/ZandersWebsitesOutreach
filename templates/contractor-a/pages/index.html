ADUs & in-law apartments

Room for family. Income for you.

An ADU is a second, smaller home on your lot — a backyard cottage, an apartment over the garage, or an in-law suite. Massachusetts now lets most homeowners build one without a special permit, and we build them start to finish.

  • A place for aging parents or grown kids, close but separate
  • Long-term rental income to help with the mortgage
  • Up to 900 sq ft by right in most MA single-family zones
  • We handle the permits, foundation, framing and finishes
