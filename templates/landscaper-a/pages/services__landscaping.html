Questions

Common questions about landscaping

What plants do well around here?

Hydrangeas, bayberry, beach plum, inkberry, Rosa rugosa, switchgrass and many native perennials handle sandy soil and salt air. We match plants to how exposed your property is.

When is the best time to plant?

Spring and early fall. Fall is often the better of the two here: the soil is warm, rain is more reliable and plants root in before summer heat.

Seed or sod for a new lawn?

Seed costs less and establishes well in early fall. Sod gives you a lawn right away but costs more and needs steady watering for the first weeks.

Do you look after seasonal and rental homes?

Yes. Cleanups and regular maintenance can be scheduled so the property is ready before you or your guests arrive.
