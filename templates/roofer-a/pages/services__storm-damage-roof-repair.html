Signs You Need Storm Damage & Insurance Claims

  • Shingles missing or scattered in the yard after high winds
  • Dents or bruising on shingles, gutters, vents or siding after hail
  • A tree or large limb that came down on the roof
  • New leaks or ceiling stains after a storm
  • Lifted or bent flashing, damaged ridge caps or vents
  • Siding blown off or cracked during the storm

Storm Damage & Insurance Claims FAQs

Will insurance pay for my roof?

Homeowners policies generally cover sudden storm damage like wind, hail and falling trees, but not normal wear and tear. Coverage depends on your specific policy — we document the damage so your insurer has what they need to decide.

Should I call my insurance company or a roofer first?

If water is coming in, call us first to secure the roof — most policies require you to prevent further damage. Then report the claim. We'll give you documentation to support it.

Do you work with my adjuster?

Yes. We can meet the adjuster at your property and walk the roof with them so the full scope of damage is recorded.

How fast can you tarp my roof?

For active leaks we prioritize emergency tarping. Response time depends on weather and how many calls come in after a big storm, so call as early as you can.
