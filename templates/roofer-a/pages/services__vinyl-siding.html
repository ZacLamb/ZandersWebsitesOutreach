Vinyl Siding Installation

Low-maintenance vinyl siding installation and replacement that boosts curb appeal, cuts upkeep and helps protect your home from New England weather.

Call [[PHONE]]

If you're tired of scraping and repainting every few years, vinyl siding is the easy answer. Modern vinyl holds its color, resists moisture and impact, and only needs an occasional wash.

[[NAME]] installs and replaces vinyl siding on single-family homes, multi-families and commercial buildings. Pairing new siding with a new roof is also a smart way to update your whole exterior in one project.

Signs It's Time for New Siding

  • Peeling paint or rotting wood clapboards
  • Cracked, warped or loose siding after storms
  • Faded siding that hurts curb appeal
  • Drafts or high heating bills
  • Preparing a home to sell

Our Vinyl Siding Installation Process

  1. Free estimateWe measure the house and help you choose profile, color and trim.
  2. Prep the wallsRemove old siding where needed, repair sheathing, install house wrap.
  3. Install siding & trimSiding, corners, J-channel, soffit and fascia installed to allow for expansion.
  4. Final clean-upDebris hauled away and a walkthrough with you.

What Affects the Price?

Every job is priced after a free on-site look, and your estimate is written and itemized. The main factors are:

  • Square footage of wall area
  • Siding profile and grade
  • Removal of old siding
  • Trim, soffit and fascia work
  • Number of stories

Vinyl Siding Installation FAQs

Can you install siding over my old siding?

Sometimes, but removing old siding lets us find and fix rot and install proper house wrap. We'll recommend what's right for your house.

How long does vinyl siding last?

Quality vinyl siding commonly lasts decades with minimal maintenance — mostly an occasional wash.

Can you do roofing and siding together?

Yes, and it's often the most efficient way to update your exterior — one crew, one schedule, one point of contact.
