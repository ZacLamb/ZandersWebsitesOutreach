Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and re-roof quality shingle systems, installed by a local crew.

A new roof is the biggest exterior investment most homeowners ever make, so it should be done once and done right. [[NAME]] tears your old roof down to the deck, inspects and replaces any rotted sheathing, and installs a complete roofing system — ice and water shield, synthetic underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ventilation and shingles — not just a new top layer.

We install quality shingle systems architectural shingles, the two most widely used shingle lines in New England, and we build every roof for what our winters actually do: heavy snow loads, nor'easter winds and the freeze-thaw cycles that create ice dams along the eaves.

Signs You Need Roof Replacement

  • Shingles that are curling, cupping, cracked or missing in several areas
  • Bald patches or lots of granules collecting in your gutters
  • A roof that is 20–25+ years old, or two layers of old shingles
  • Sagging rooflines or soft spots when walked on
  • Recurring leaks in different spots, or daylight visible in the attic
  • Ice dams forming every winter along the same eaves

Our Roof Replacement Process

  1. Free on-site estimateWe measure the roof, check the attic and decking from the inside, and give you a written, itemized quote — no pressure, no "sign today" pricing.
  2. Choose your systemPick your shingle line and color. We explain underlayment, ventilation and warranty options in plain English.
  3. Permit & schedulingWe pull the building permit with your town and schedule the job, typically within 1–2 weeks of signing depending on season and materials.
  4. Tear-off & deck repairOld roofing is removed down to the deck, landscaping is protected with tarps, and any rotted plywood is replaced.
  5. Install the full systemIce and water shield at eaves and valleys, synthetic underlayment, new flashing, drip edge, ridge vent and shingles.
  6. Clean-up & walkthroughMagnetic sweep for nails, debris hauled away, and a final walkthrough with you before we call it done.

Options We Offer

Architectural asphalt shingles

The most popular choice in Massachusetts and New Hampshire — dimensional look, high wind ratings and a long lifespan for the price.

Designer / premium shingles

Heavier profiles that mimic slate or cedar shake for historic and high-end homes.

Low-slope & flat sections

Porches, dormers and additions get EPDM rubber or another membrane rated for low pitch — shingles leak on flat sections.

What Affects the Price?

Every job is priced after a free on-site look, and your estimate is written and itemized. The main factors are:

  • Roof size and number of squares
  • Pitch / steepness and how many stories
  • Number of existing layers to remove
  • Amount of rotted decking to replace
  • Chimneys, skylights, dormers and valleys
  • Shingle line and warranty level chosen

Roof Replacement FAQs

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes are torn off and re-roofed in 1–2 days. Larger or more complex roofs, multi-families and jobs with a lot of deck repair can take longer — we'll give you a realistic timeline in your estimate.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. In Massachusetts a building permit is required for re-roofing, and we handle the permit with your town building department. New Hampshire towns have their own requirements and we follow them.

Can you roof over my existing shingles?

Sometimes it's allowed, but we usually recommend a full tear-off. It lets us inspect and repair the deck, install ice and water shield properly, and it generally protects your manufacturer warranty.

What happens if it rains during my job?

We watch the forecast closely and only open up as much roof as we can close the same day. If weather changes unexpectedly, we tarp and secure the roof so no water gets in.

How much does a new roof cost?

It depends on size, pitch, layers, decking and materials. We give free, written estimates so you know the real number before any work starts.
