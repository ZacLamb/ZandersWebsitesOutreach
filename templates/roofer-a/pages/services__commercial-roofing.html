Signs You Need Commercial Roofing

  • Leaks over occupied space or inventory
  • Ponding water and clogged drains
  • Aging membrane with open seams
  • Upcoming property sale, refinance or insurance inspection
  • You want a maintenance plan instead of emergency calls

Our Commercial Roofing Process

  1. Site assessmentRoof walk, core checks where needed, photos and a written condition report.
  2. Options & budgetRepair, restore or replace — priced so you can plan capital spending.
  3. Scheduled installWork planned around tenants and business hours.
  4. Ongoing maintenanceSeasonal inspections and drain clearing to extend roof life.

Options We Offer

EPDM & TPO membranes

Single-ply systems for flat and low-slope commercial roofs.

Modified bitumen

Durable multi-layer system for high-traffic roofs.

Metal roofing

Long-life option for certain commercial and agricultural buildings.

Repairs & maintenance

Leak repairs, seam work and preventive maintenance.

What Affects the Price?

Every job is priced after a free on-site look, and your estimate is written and itemized. The main factors are:

  • Roof area and system type
  • Tear-off and insulation requirements
  • Drains, HVAC curbs and penetrations
  • Access, staging and scheduling constraints

Commercial Roofing FAQs

Do you work on multi-family buildings?

Yes — triple-deckers and other multi-family buildings make up a big part of the housing stock around here, and we work on them regularly.

Can you work while my business stays open?

In most cases, yes. We plan staging, noise and access around your hours.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

We can set up seasonal inspections and drain clearing so small issues get caught before they become leaks.
