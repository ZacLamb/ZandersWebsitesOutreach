Roof Inspections

Honest roof inspections for homeowners, buyers and sellers — with photos and a straight answer on whether you need repair, replacement, or nothing at all.

Call [[PHONE]]

We recommend having your roof looked at about once a year and after any major storm. Small problems — a lifted shingle, a cracked pipe boot, a gap in chimney flashing — are cheap to fix early and expensive to ignore.

[[NAME]] inspections cover the roof surface, flashing, ventilation, gutters and the attic underside. You get a plain-English summary and photos of anything that needs attention.

When to Schedule an Inspection

  • Buying or selling a home
  • Your roof is 15+ years old
  • After a nor'easter, hailstorm or high winds
  • Before winter, to head off ice dams
  • You noticed granules, stains or drafts

Our Roof Inspections Process

  1. Exterior roof checkShingles, flashing, valleys, vents, chimneys, skylights and gutters.
  2. Attic checkSigns of leaks, ventilation, insulation and decking condition.
  3. Photos & summaryWhat we found, what's urgent, what can wait.
  4. Straight recommendationRepair, replace, or leave it alone — whichever is honestly right.

Roof Inspections FAQs

How often should I get my roof inspected?

At least once a year and after major storms. Fall is a great time — you can fix problems before snow and ice arrive.

Will you try to sell me a new roof?

No. Our reputation in [[CITY]] was built over years on honest recommendations. If your roof is fine, we'll tell you.

What do you look for in the attic?

Water stains, wet or compressed insulation, frost, mold, daylight through the deck, and whether ventilation is adequate.
