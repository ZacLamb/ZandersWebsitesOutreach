Low-slope roofs can't shed water the way a pitched shingle roof does, so they need a true waterproof membrane. [[NAME]] installs EPDM rubber roofing, the long-proven choice for flat and low-slope roofs on porches, dormers, additions, triple-deckers and commercial buildings throughout the [[CITY]]-area.

We pay close attention to the parts that usually fail: seams, edges, drains, scuppers and the transitions where a flat section meets a pitched roof or wall.

Signs You Need Flat & Rubber Roofing (EPDM)

  • Ponding water that sits for days after rain
  • Blisters, bubbles or cracks in the membrane
  • Leaks under porches, dormers or flat additions
  • Old tar-and-gravel or rolled roofing that keeps failing
  • Separated seams or loose edges

Flat & Rubber Roofing (EPDM) FAQs

Can you put shingles on my flat porch roof?

No — shingles need enough pitch to shed water. On flat or very low-slope sections they leak. A membrane like EPDM is the right material.

How long does an EPDM roof last?

A properly installed EPDM roof commonly lasts decades. The seams and edge details matter most, which is where we spend extra care.

Do you repair flat roofs too?

Yes. Many flat roof leaks are seam or flashing problems that can be repaired without replacing the whole roof.
