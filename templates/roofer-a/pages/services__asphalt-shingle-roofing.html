What Affects the Price?

Every job is priced after a free on-site look, and your estimate is written and itemized. The main factors are:

  • Shingle line and profile
  • Roof size and complexity
  • Tear-off layers
  • Ventilation upgrades

Asphalt Shingle Roofing FAQs

How long do asphalt shingles last in New England?

Quality architectural shingles commonly last 20–30 years here depending on ventilation, sun exposure and maintenance. Good attic ventilation is one of the biggest factors.

Are architectural shingles worth it over three-tab?

For most homes, yes. They look better, resist wind better and usually last longer for a modest price difference.
