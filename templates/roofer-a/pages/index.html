Good questions

Things neighbors often ask us

How much does a new roof cost?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof, how many layers need to come off, how much decking needs replacing, and which shingle system you choose. We give free, written, itemized estimates so you see the real number before anything starts.

How soon can you start my project?

We aim to start most projects within 1–2 weeks after the contract is signed, depending on the season and material availability. Emergency repairs can often be scheduled sooner.

What happens if it rains during my roof job?

We take every precaution to protect your home. We only open as much roof as we can close the same day, and if unexpected weather comes in we use tarps and covers so no additional damage occurs.

Can you help with insurance claims for storm damage?

Yes. We document roof damage with photos and measurements, provide a written report, and can meet your adjuster on site so the full scope of damage is recorded.

How often should I schedule a roof inspection?

At least once a year and after major storms. Fall is ideal so problems get fixed before snow and ice dams arrive.

What causes ice dams and how do I stop them?

Ice dams form when heat escaping into the attic melts snow on the upper roof, and the water refreezes at the colder eaves. The long-term fix is good attic insulation and ventilation; ice and water shield along the eaves (which we install on every new roof) protects against leaks when they do form.
