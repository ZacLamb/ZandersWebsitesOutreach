Signs You Need Roof Repair

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls, especially after heavy rain or snowmelt
  • Missing, lifted or creased shingles after a windstorm
  • Leaks around the chimney, skylight, vent pipes or where the roof meets a wall
  • Drips or frost in the attic, damp insulation or a musty smell
  • Rusted or loose flashing and cracked rubber pipe boots
  • Ice dams that pushed water under the shingles last winter

What Affects the Price?

Every job is priced after a free on-site look, and your estimate is written and itemized. The main factors are:

  • Location and access of the problem area
  • Roof pitch and height
  • Materials needed (shingles, flashing, membrane)
  • Whether decking underneath is damaged
  • Emergency vs. scheduled service

Roof Repair FAQs

Should I repair or replace my roof?

If the roof is under about 15–20 years old and the damage is localized, a repair usually makes sense. If it's older, has widespread wear, or keeps leaking in new spots, replacement is often the better money. We'll show you what we see and let you decide.

Can you match my existing shingles?

In most cases we can match closely. Older or discontinued colors can be harder, and we'll tell you upfront how close the match will be.

Do you do emergency roof repairs?

Yes. For active leaks and storm damage we can usually tarp and secure the roof quickly to stop further damage, then schedule the permanent repair.

Why is my ceiling stain far from the leak?

Water runs along rafters, sheathing and insulation before it drips. That's why we inspect from the attic as well as the roof to find the true entry point.
