Whole-Home Surge Protection in [[TOWN]]

Panel-level surge protection for the electronics and appliances that matter.

Schedule Service [[PHONE]]

A single lightning strike or utility surge can take out a furnace control board, a refrigerator, or a home's electronics in an instant. A panel-level surge protector is installed once and protects everything downstream of it, every time.

What's Included

  • Panel-level whole-home surge protectors
  • HVAC & appliance circuit protection
  • Home electronics & smart device protection
  • Surge protector testing & replacement
  • Pairing with generator & transfer switch installs

One install, ongoing protection

Unlike a power strip surge protector that only covers what's plugged into it, a whole-home unit is wired directly into your panel and protects every circuit in the house — including hardwired equipment like HVAC systems that plug-in protectors can't reach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a whole-home surge protector worth it?

For homes with modern HVAC systems, appliances, and electronics, yes — the cost of a single fried control board or appliance often exceeds the cost of the protector itself.
