Real diagnosis, not guesswork

Electrical symptoms can be misleading — a flickering light in one room is sometimes a loose connection two rooms away. Our team traces problems back to their source with a meter in hand, not a parts-swapping approach that runs up your bill.

Same standard, every call

Whether it's a single dead outlet or a house that lost power after a storm, every repair gets the same attention to detail — proper torque on connections, correct wire gauge, and a panel that's left safer than it was found.

Frequently Asked Questions

My breaker keeps tripping — is that an emergency?

A breaker that trips repeatedly is your panel doing its job and protecting the circuit. It shouldn't be ignored, but it's not always a same-day emergency. Call and our team will help you figure out if it can wait or needs immediate attention.

Do you work on older fuse panels?

Yes, our team services older fuse and breaker panels and can advise on whether a repair or a full panel upgrade is the smarter long-term move.
