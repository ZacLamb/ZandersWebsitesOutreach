Know if your panel is a liability

Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels — common in homes built before the 1980s — have well-documented breaker failure issues and are frequently flagged by home inspectors and insurance carriers. If you're not sure what's in your panel, our team will take a look and give you a straight answer.

Built for what's next

A panel upgrade is the right time to plan ahead — extra breaker space for a future EV charger, a generator interlock, or a hot tub circuit costs far less to rough in now than to add later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?

Frequent breaker trips, a panel that's full with no room for new circuits, warm or discolored breakers, or a Federal Pacific/Zinsco panel are all signs it's time for an evaluation.

Will I be without power during the upgrade?

Yes, briefly — typically a few hours while the utility disconnects and reconnects service. Our team schedules the outage window with you in advance so it's not a surprise.
