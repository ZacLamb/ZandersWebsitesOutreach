Designed, not just installed

Recessed lighting placed on a grid instead of over the counter or seating you actually use is a common mistake. Our team lays out fixtures based on how the room is used, not just how it's easiest to wire.

Waterfront-ready exterior lighting

Homes around here take more weather than most. Our team installs marine-grade, corrosion-resistant fixtures for docks, decks, and exterior lighting so you're not replacing corroded hardware every couple of seasons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you convert my existing fixtures to LED?

In most cases, yes — either with an LED-compatible bulb swap or a full fixture retrofit, depending on your existing wiring and dimmer compatibility.

Do you install smart lighting and dimmer switches?

Yes, including smart switches and dimmers compatible with most major smart home systems, wired properly with a neutral where required.
