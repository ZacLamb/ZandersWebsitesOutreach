Wiring you don't have to think about again

Old cloth wiring, undersized circuits, and DIY additions from a previous owner are some of the most common issues found in local homes. Our team inspects the full picture before quoting a fix, so you're not paying to patch a symptom while the underlying problem stays put.

Built for renovations

Adding a room, finishing a basement, or redoing a kitchen? Electrical is easiest to get right before the drywall goes up. Our team works directly with homeowners and contractors to rough-in circuits, place outlets where you'll actually use them, and pass inspection the first time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for residential electrical work?

Most work beyond simple fixture swaps requires a permit and inspection in Massachusetts. Our team pulls the permit and handles the inspection as part of the job so you don't have to.

Can you match my home's existing wiring?

Yes. On older homes with cloth or aluminum wiring, our team will walk you through your options — from a targeted repair to a full rewire — with an honest read on what's actually necessary.
